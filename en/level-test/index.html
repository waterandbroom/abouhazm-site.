Free English level test

Up to 36 questions, about 10 minutes. The test adapts: it stops when the questions become too difficult.

This test measures grammar and vocabulary. Speaking and writing are assessed in your first lesson, so your final level can differ by one step.

The six levels

LevelNameWhat you can doThe step up
A1BeginnerUnderstands and uses familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases; can introduce themselves and ask simple questions.—
A2ElementaryHandles simple, routine exchanges: shopping, directions, family, work basics; describes things in simple terms.From isolated phrases to simple connected sentences.
B1IntermediateCopes with most situations while travelling; describes experiences, plans and opinions; understands the main points of clear speech.From routine exchanges to telling stories and giving reasons.
B2Upper-intermediateFollows complex texts and fast speech; interacts fluently with native speakers; writes clear, detailed texts on many subjects.From coping to fluent, spontaneous communication.
C1AdvancedUnderstands demanding texts and implicit meaning; expresses ideas fluently and flexibly for work and study.From fluent to flexible, precise and professional.
C2ProficiencyUnderstands virtually everything heard or read; expresses subtle shades of meaning precisely, even in complex situations.Near-native precision and nuance.
